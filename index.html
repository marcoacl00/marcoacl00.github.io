About me

Hello! I am a PhD student in particle physics, currently based at IPhT-Saclay for a research stay until January 2027. My work is supervised by Guilherme Milhano (LIP/IST), Alba Soto-Ontoso (U. Granada) and Gregory Soyez (IPhT). I also spent one year of my PhD at LIP-Lisbon. I did my undergraduate studies at University of Porto and my master's at Instituto Superior Técnico, University of Lisbon.

My research focuses on QCD phenomenology, with an emphasis in the perturbative evolution of particles both in vacuum and in a dense medium. Alongside this, a major interest of mine is in scientific computing, namely numerical simulation methods, high performance computing and Machine Learning tools, which I employ in my PhD research on a regular basis.

I am also interested in the training of young undergraduate students, and supporting independent, curiosity-driven projects - especially those that connect different areas of physics and computation.
